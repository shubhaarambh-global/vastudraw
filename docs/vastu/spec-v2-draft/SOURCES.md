# Phase 26B source notes

This draft knowledge base is deliberately not presented as a single universal
or statutory interpretation of Vastu Shastra.

Seed references include:

1. Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology — residential
   project report listing room-placement Vastu parameters.
2. Sushant University — Vaastu Shastra guidance.
3. Veer Narmad South Gujarat University — publication on Indian traditional
   architectural wisdom and directional room associations.
4. "Applied Principles of Indian Vastu in Contemporary Residential Architecture
   for Urban Areas" — used particularly to preserve climate/context variation.
5. Karnataka Sanskrit University — Vaastu course syllabus, including open-space
   and directional-load concepts.

Where sources disagree (notably toilets, bedrooms by type/climate, living and
dining placement), the catalog marks `source_disagreement=true` and permits
contextual downgrading. The model is not authorized to erase the disagreement.

Before calling this catalog production-frozen, a human Vastu expert should
review every source, rule, allowed adjustment range, and suggestion.
